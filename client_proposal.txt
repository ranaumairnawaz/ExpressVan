============================================================
  REMOVAL BOOKING WEBSITE — PROPOSED IMPROVEMENTS
  Prepared for: [Client Name]
  Date: October 2026
============================================================

This document outlines the improvements we plan to make to your
removal booking website. These changes will make the website faster,
easier to use for your customers, and easier to manage for your team.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — BOOKING EXPERIENCE (WHAT THE CUSTOMER SEES)
------------------------------------------------------------

1. LIVE PRICE CALCULATOR ON BOOKING PAGE
   Currently, customers only see the price AFTER they fill in all
   their details and click "Next". We will add a live price display
   that updates automatically as they select their van, helpers, date,
   and addresses. No more surprises at the end.

2. SMOOTHER BOOKING JOURNEY (NO MORE PAGE RELOADS)
   Right now the booking has 3 separate pages that fully reload every
   time. We will make it feel like one smooth continuous form where
   customers move between steps without the page jumping or resetting.
   This makes the process feel faster and more professional.

3. ROUTE MAP SHOWN DURING BOOKING
   We will show customers a live map with their pickup and drop-off
   route during the booking process, so they can confirm the right
   addresses before paying.

4. BETTER INVENTORY / ITEMS LIST
   Instead of customers typing what they want to move, we will give
   them a proper checklist with common items (bedroom furniture,
   washing machine, boxes, etc.) and quantities. This also helps
   your team know exactly what to expect on the day.

5. RESCHEDULE & CANCELLATION OPTION
   At the moment there is no way for customers to request a reschedule
   or cancellation online. We will add a simple form for this, with
   admin approval before any changes are confirmed.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — CUSTOMER ACCOUNT & COMMUNICATION
------------------------------------------------------------

6. CUSTOMER ACCOUNT PORTAL
   Customers will be able to create a simple account where they can:
   - View their current and past bookings
   - Download their invoices
   - Request changes to their booking
   - Track the status of their job
   This removes the current clunky OTP (one-time code) system.

7. SMS TEXT MESSAGE NOTIFICATIONS
   We will add text message (SMS) alerts to customers at key points:
   - When their booking is confirmed
   - A reminder the day before their move
   - When the van is on the way
   - When a payment reminder is due
   This dramatically improves the customer experience and reduces
   no-shows and missed payments.

8. WHATSAPP BUTTON
   A WhatsApp chat button will be added to the website so customers
   can quickly reach you. Booking confirmations can also optionally
   be sent via WhatsApp.

9. AUTOMATIC REVIEW REQUEST
   After a job is completed, the system will automatically send the
   customer an email 24 hours later asking them to leave a review on
   Google. This runs itself — no action needed from your team.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — PAYMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

10. MORE PAYMENT OPTIONS
    Currently only card payment through Stripe is available. We will
    add:
    - PayPal (many customers prefer this)
    - Bank Transfer / BACS (for business clients)
    - "Pay on the Day" / Cash option (marked as unpaid until confirmed)

11. CONFIGURABLE DEPOSIT PERCENTAGE
    Right now the deposit is fixed at 30% and cannot be changed without
    a developer. We will add a simple setting in your admin panel where
    you can change the deposit percentage yourself (e.g. to 25% or 50%).

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 — ADMIN PANEL (WHAT YOUR TEAM SEES)
------------------------------------------------------------

12. REVENUE & ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
    We will add a proper dashboard on the admin homepage showing:
    - Total revenue this week / month / year
    - Number of bookings by status (pending, confirmed, completed, etc.)
    - Most popular van types
    - Busiest days / times
    - Revenue by area / postcode zone
    All displayed with clear charts and numbers.

13. BETTER ORDER SEARCH & FILTERING
    Right now all orders are in one big table. We will add filters so
    your team can quickly find orders by:
    - Date range
    - Status (unpaid, confirmed, completed, cancelled)
    - Customer name or email
    - Van type
    - Postcode or city

14. CREW / DRIVER ASSIGNMENT
    We will add the ability to assign a specific driver or crew member
    to each job. The crew member will:
    - Receive an email/SMS notification when assigned
    - Be able to view their jobs for the day
    - Mark a job as started and completed
    This replaces manual WhatsApp coordination with your drivers.

15. DRIVER / CREW MOBILE-FRIENDLY VIEW
    A simple mobile-friendly page for drivers to see their jobs for
    the day, get navigation directions, and mark jobs done — all from
    their phone without needing an app download.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 — PERFORMANCE & RELIABILITY (BEHIND THE SCENES)
------------------------------------------------------------

16. FASTER PAGE LOADING
    We will optimise how the website fetches data from the database
    and caches common information (like Google Maps distances) so
    pages load much faster — especially during busy periods.

17. EMAIL SENDING IN THE BACKGROUND
    Currently when a customer books, the website waits while it sends
    emails before completing the page. We will move email sending to
    a background process so customers get an instant response and
    emails still go out reliably.

18. WEBSITE SPEED & MOBILE OPTIMISATION
    A full review of loading times on mobile devices, with improvements
    to make the booking pages fast and smooth on phones — where most
    customers are likely visiting from.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 — QUICK WINS (SMALL BUT IMPACTFUL)
------------------------------------------------------------

19. PAYMENT REMINDER EMAILS (ALREADY PARTLY DONE)
    Improve the automated payment reminder emails with clearer
    formatting and direct payment links so customers can pay in
    one click.

20. BOOKING CONFIRMATION EMAIL IMPROVEMENTS
    Update the booking confirmation email to look more professional,
    include the route summary, and have a clear "View My Booking"
    button.

21. INVOICE PDF DOWNLOAD
    Allow customers and admins to download a clean PDF invoice for
    each booking directly from the website.

22. GOOGLE REVIEWS WIDGET ON WEBSITE
    Show your latest Google Reviews automatically on the homepage to
    build trust with new visitors.

------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY — WHAT THIS ACHIEVES
------------------------------------------------------------

BETTER FOR CUSTOMERS:
  - Smoother, faster, more modern booking experience
  - Text message and WhatsApp updates
  - Self-service account to manage bookings
  - More payment options

BETTER FOR YOUR BUSINESS:
  - Clear revenue and performance dashboard
  - Driver/crew job assignment built in
  - Automatic review collection
  - Configurable settings you control yourself

BETTER TECHNICALLY:
  - Faster, more reliable website
  - Fewer manual tasks for your team
  - Easier to grow and add features in the future
